# Video Downloader - How to use

## Install
1. Download **VideoDownloader-Setup.exe** and double-click it.
2. Windows may show a blue box: **"Windows protected your PC"**. This is normal for new apps.
   Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Press Next until it finishes. You'll get a shortcut on your desktop.

## Download a video
1. Copy the video link from your browser.
2. Open **Video Downloader** and press **Paste** (or paste into the box).
3. Choose **Quality** (or **Audio only (MP3)** for music).
4. Choose where to save with **Browse...** (the Downloads folder is the default).
5. Press **Download**. When it says "Finished", press **Open folder**.

Optional: type a **File name**, or paste several links (one per line) to download them one after another.

## If something goes wrong
- **"Update downloader"**: press this first. YouTube and X change often and updates fix most problems.
- **"Needs you to be signed in" / age-restricted:** sign in to the site in your browser, then choose that
  browser under **Use browser login** and try again. Close the browser if it still fails.
- **"Couldn't reach the site":** check your internet connection.
- **Video not available:** it may be private, removed or blocked in your country.

## Please
Only download videos you own, that are free to use, or that you have permission to save.
