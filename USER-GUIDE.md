# Video Downloader - How to use

## Install
1. Download **VideoDownloader-Setup.exe** and double-click it.
2. Windows may show a blue box: **"Windows protected your PC"**. This is normal for new apps.
   Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Press Next until it finishes. You'll get a shortcut on your desktop.
4. The first time you open the app, read the short notice and tick **I understand**.

## Download a video
1. Copy the video link from your browser.
2. In the app, press **Paste** (or paste into the link box). After a moment a **preview** with the picture and title appears.
3. Choose the **Quality** (or **Audio only (MP3)** for music) and where to save. Downloads go to your Downloads folder by default.
4. Press **Add to queue** (or press Enter). The download starts by itself.

You can keep adding more links. Videos download **one at a time, in order**, and each has its own progress bar.

## The Queue
- **Cancel** stops a video. **Retry** tries again if something failed.
- **Show file** opens the folder with the finished video selected.
- **Remove** takes it off the list, and **Clear finished** tidies the whole list. Your videos are never deleted.

## The History tab
Every finished download is listed here with its date. Press **Show file** to find it again.

## Light and dark theme
Use the **System / Light / Dark** switch in the top right corner.

## If something goes wrong
- **Update downloader** (bottom right): press this first. YouTube and X change often and updates fix most problems. The app also checks by itself every time it starts.
- **"Needs you to be signed in" / age-restricted:** sign in to the site in your browser, then choose that browser under **Browser login** and try again. Close the browser if it still fails.
- **"Couldn't reach the site":** check your internet connection.
- **Video not available:** it may be private, removed or blocked in your country.

## Please
Only download videos you own, that are free to use, or that you have permission to save.
