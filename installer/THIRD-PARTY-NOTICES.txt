Video Downloader bundles the following third-party software.

yt-dlp
  License: The Unlicense (public domain dedication)
  Project: https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp

FFmpeg (LGPL build, unmodified)
  License: GNU Lesser General Public License v2.1 or later
  Project: https://ffmpeg.org
  Build used: https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds
  FFmpeg source code is available from the links above. ffmpeg.exe and
  ffprobe.exe are separate programs inside the "bin" folder and can be
  replaced with your own copies.

Deno (JavaScript runtime, unmodified)
  License: MIT License
  Project: https://deno.com
  Used by yt-dlp to read YouTube pages so that all video qualities are available.
